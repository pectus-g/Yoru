CORE CONCEPT
High Concept
Play as Yoru, a two-tailed Nekomata navigating purgatory. Every moral choice is literally visible on your body through glowing rings on your tails. Your decisions shape both your appearance and the entire world around you.
Unique Selling Points
Visible Karma: Rings on tails show your moral path (left = dark/combat, right = light/persuasion)
Dual Forms: Transform between Nekomata (cat) and Old Woman (Tomoe) to see emotional auras
Balance System: Environment changes dynamically based on ring distribution (7 possible world states)
Buddhist Themes: Each boss represents one of the 10 Fetters (attachments) from Buddhist philosophy
Authentic Japanese Folklore: All bosses based on real yokai with cultural respect
Core Themes
Grief and acceptance
Duality (life/death, light/dark, forgiveness/vengeance)
Breaking attachments to find peace
Memory and identity
The weight of choices

PERSUASION SYSTEM
Emotional Threading Mini-Game
CONCEPT: Weave a thread of understanding through the enemy's tangled emotions to reach their heart.
How It Works
Visual:
Screen shows enemy's "emotional web" - like a constellation/neural network
Multiple emotion nodes (glowing orbs) connected by potential paths
Each node has a color matching emotional state
Center = HEART (goal)

Example for Yuki-onna (GRIEF):

    [ANGER-Red]     [FEAR-Yellow]
         ↓         ↙
    [GRIEF-Blue] → [GUILT-Purple] → [HOPE-White] → ❤️ HEART
         ↑         ↘
    [DESPAIR-Gray]  [REGRET-Purple]
Gameplay:
Player sees 8-10 emotion nodes scattered on screen
Must draw a line connecting nodes in correct order
Click/drag mouse or use arrow keys to trace path
Some connections work (nodes light up green)
Some connections break thread (nodes flash red, try again)
Reach heart = Persuasion success!
Limited attempts (3 tries per boss)
Difficulty Scaling:
BASED ON DARK RING COUNT:

Easy (0-2 dark rings):
- 3 correct nodes in straight line
- 5 total nodes (2 wrong)
- Clear emotional logic

Medium (3-5 dark rings):
- 5 correct nodes
- 8 total nodes (3 wrong)
- Some branching paths

Hard (6-8 dark rings):
- 7 correct nodes
- 12 total nodes (5 wrong)
- Multiple dead-end paths
- Time limit (60 seconds)

Very Hard (9-10 dark rings):
- 8 correct nodes
- 15 total nodes (7 wrong)
- Complex web, many dead ends
- Time limit (45 seconds)
Example Correct Paths:
Yuki-onna (Grief): GRIEF → GUILT → REGRET → HOPE → HEART
Oni (Rage): RAGE → EMPATHY → CALM → UNDERSTANDING → HEART
Gashadokuro (Despair): DESPAIR → ACKNOWLEDGMENT → PURPOSE → HOPE → HEART
Success/Failure
Success:
Thread reaches heart
Beautiful particle effect
Enemy calms, becomes peaceful
Drops blue soul orb (persuasion)
+1 right tail ring potential
Failure (3 attempts used):
Thread cannot reach heart
Enemy becomes hostile
Forces combat encounter
If defeated in combat, drops red soul orb
+1 left tail ring potential
Partial Success (2 attempts):
Can try again with hints
Some nodes glow slightly (guidance)

COMBAT SYSTEM
COMBAT SYSTEM
[Updated February 2026]

This section explains how combat works in YORU. The combat is inspired by Zelda and God of War — it should feel smooth, satisfying, and empowering. Players should feel like a powerful cat spirit, not struggle against the controls.


COMBAT PHILOSOPHY

The combat in YORU follows these principles:

1. NO STAMINA FOR COMBAT
   Unlike Dark Souls, there is no stamina bar that limits attacks. Players can attack freely. Only climbing uses energy.

2. CLEAR ENEMY TELEGRAPHS
   Enemies signal their attacks before hitting. This gives players time to dodge or parry. The game rewards skill but doesn't punish casual players.

3. DUAL PATHS
   Every enemy (including bosses) can be defeated through Combat OR through Persuasion/Dialogue. Both paths give equal rewards.

4. CAT SPIRIT THEME
   Yoru is a Nekomata — a two-tailed cat spirit. Movements should feel agile, graceful, and feline. The signature power is Necromancy (raising the dead).


THREE RESOURCE SYSTEMS

YORU uses three separate resources. Each has a different purpose:

RESOURCE 1: PEACHES (Health)

What it is: Your health, displayed as peaches on screen.

How it works:
• 1 Peach = 4 HP (hit points)
• You start with 3 Peaches (12 HP total)
• Maximum is 8 Peaches (32 HP total)
• When you take damage, you lose HP
• When HP reaches 0, you die

How to get more:
• Eat food items to heal
• Use Healing Whisper ability (Light path)
• Use Eclipse Mercy ultimate (Light path)
• Add Health Sockets at the Secret Tree (permanent increase)


RESOURCE 2: ENERGY (For Climbing Only)

What it is: Your climbing stamina.

How it works:
• Displayed as energy bars
• ONLY used when climbing walls/surfaces
• NOT used for combat or abilities
• Depletes while climbing, regenerates when resting

How to get more:
• Wait for it to regenerate
• Use consumable items
• Add Energy Sockets at the Secret Tree (permanent increase)


RESOURCE 3: SHINE (For Necromancy Only)

What it is: Your spiritual energy for maintaining ghost puppets.

How it works:
• Displayed as shine bars
• ONLY used when you have a summoned ghost puppet active
• Drains CONTINUOUSLY while your puppet exists
• When Shine runs out, your puppet disappears
• Regenerates when no puppet is active

How to get more:
• Wait for it to regenerate (when no puppet active)
• Use consumable items
• Add Shine Sockets at the Secret Tree (permanent increase)


SOCKET CHOICE

After defeating each boss and returning to the Secret Tree, you choose ONE:
• +1 Health Socket (more Peaches - lets you take more hits)
• +1 Energy Socket (more climbing stamina)
• +1 Shine Socket (puppets last longer)

This choice is permanent for that playthrough. Choose based on your playstyle.


STANCE SYSTEM

Yoru has two stances that switch automatically:

2-LEG STANCE (Standing/Walking)
• Normal movement speed
• Full damage on attacks (100%)
• Backflip dodge (more invincibility frames)
• Used when: standing still or walking

4-LEG STANCE (Sprinting)
• Faster movement (+30% speed)
• Reduced damage per hit (85%)
• Roll dodge (longer distance)
• Used when: holding sprint button

The stance changes automatically based on whether you're sprinting or not. You don't need to think about it — just know that sprinting makes you faster but slightly weaker.


BASIC COMBO SYSTEM

The basic attack is a 3-hit combo:

HIT 1: PAW SWIPE
• Animation: Single paw swipe forward
• Damage: 10
• Duration: 0.4 seconds
• Targets: One enemy

HIT 2: DOUBLE PAW
• Animation: Both paws strike together
• Damage: 20
• Duration: 0.5 seconds
• Targets: One enemy

HIT 3: FORWARD SPIN
• Animation: Yoru spins forward like a Tasmanian devil
• Damage: 35
• Duration: 0.7 seconds
• Targets: ONE enemy only (not area damage!)

TOTAL COMBO: 65 damage in 1.6 seconds

COMBO WINDOW: You have 1 second between each click to continue the combo. If you wait longer than 1 second, the combo resets.

IMPORTANT NOTE ABOUT HIT 3:
The spin attack only hits ONE enemy — the one you're facing. This is different from the Inferno Spiral ring ability, which hits ALL enemies around you. The spin is a rushing attack toward one target, not a spinning area attack.


HEAVY ATTACK

Hold the attack button instead of tapping:

MINIMUM CHARGE (0.5 seconds):
• Damage: 50
• Effect: Enemy staggers (briefly stunned)

MEDIUM CHARGE (1.0 seconds):
• Damage: 65
• Effect: Stagger + knockback (enemy pushed away)

MAXIMUM CHARGE (1.5 seconds):
• Damage: 80
• Effect: Stagger + guard break (ignores blocking enemies)

Use heavy attacks on tough enemies or when you have an opening.


MOVEMENT ATTACKS

POUNCE ATTACK
• How to do it: Sprint + Attack
• Damage: 40
• What it does: Yoru leaps forward and strikes. Good for closing distance quickly. Knocks down small enemies.

DIVE ATTACK
• How to do it: Attack while in the air
• Damage: 45
• What it does: Yoru plunges straight down. Primarily useful for descending from heights quickly, not combat-focused.


DODGE SYSTEM

Press dodge to avoid attacks:

BACKFLIP (Dodge + Back or No Direction):
• Distance: 2 meters (2.5m if sprinting)
• Invincibility: Frames 6-20 of animation
• Use when: You need quick escape, more safe frames

FORWARD DASH (Dodge + Forward):
• Distance: 3 meters (3.5m if sprinting)
• Invincibility: Frames 4-16 of animation
• Use when: You want to dodge through an attack

There is NO upgrade for dodge. It's skill-based — practice the timing.


PERFECT DODGE AND FLURRY RUSH

If you dodge at the EXACT moment an enemy attack would hit you (within 0.1 seconds):

What happens:
1. Time slows down to 30% speed for 2 seconds
2. You move at normal speed (everything else is slow)
3. "Flurry Rush" prompt appears
4. Mash attack button to hit up to 6 times (8 with Flurry Stone)
5. Each hit does 15 damage

Total damage: 90 (or 120 with Flurry Stone)

This is a powerful reward for skilled timing. Practice on easier enemies first.


PARRY SYSTEM

Press the parry button to block an attack:

PARRY WINDOW: 0.4 seconds (0.5 with Parry Stone)
• This means you have a small window to press parry before the attack hits

IF SUCCESSFUL:
• Enemy staggers for 1.2 seconds
• You can immediately counter-attack
• Counter damage: 60 (90 with Counter Stone)

IF YOU FAIL:
• Yoru is briefly vulnerable
• You might get hit

The parry animation is a "scared cat pose" — Yoru puffs up defensively.


DEFAULT ABILITIES (Available From Game Start)

These abilities are FREE — you have them from the beginning without needing any rings:

COMBAT ABILITIES:
1. Paw Attack (Combo 1) — 10 damage — Paw Stone adds +25%
2. TwoPaw Attack (Combo 2) — 20 damage — Paw Stone adds +25%
3. Spin Attack (Combo 3) — 35 damage — Spin Stone adds +25%
4. Heavy Attack — 50-80 damage — Heavy Stone adds +25%
5. Pounce Attack — 40 damage — Movement Stone adds +25%
6. Dive Attack — 45 damage — No stone upgrade
7. Parry Counter — 60 damage — Counter Stone adds +50%
8. Flurry Rush — 90 damage (6 hits) — Flurry Stone adds +2 hits

BASIC LEFT TAIL (Offensive):
• What it does: Fires a shadow projectile at enemies
• Default: 1 projectile, 5 damage
• With Left Tail Stone: 5 projectiles in a spread pattern
• Cooldown: 3 seconds

BASIC RIGHT TAIL (Defensive):
• What it does: Creates an air barrier that blocks attacks
• Default: Blocks 1 hit, lasts 2 seconds
• With Right Tail Stone: Blocks 3 hits, lasts 5 seconds
• Cooldown: 5 seconds
• IMPORTANT: Right tail does NO DAMAGE. It is purely defensive.


STONE SYSTEM

Stones are upgrades for your DEFAULT abilities. You equip them in slots.

THE 10 STONES:

1. PAW STONE
   • Affects: Combo 1, Combo 2, Flurry Rush hits
   • Effect: +25% damage
   • Quest to unlock: Defeat 20 kill points worth of enemies using combo attacks

2. SPIN STONE
   • Affects: Combo 3 (forward spin)
   • Effect: +25% damage
   • Quest to unlock: Defeat 15 kill points worth of enemies using spin attack

3. HEAVY STONE
   • Affects: Heavy Attack (charged)
   • Effect: +25% damage
   • Quest to unlock: Defeat 15 kill points worth of enemies using heavy attack

4. MOVEMENT STONE
   • Affects: Pounce Attack
   • Effect: +25% damage
   • Quest to unlock: Defeat 15 kill points worth of enemies using pounce

5. LEFT TAIL STONE
   • Affects: Basic Left Tail projectile
   • Effect: Fires 5 projectiles instead of 1
   • Quest to unlock: Defeat 20 kill points worth of enemies using left tail

6. RIGHT TAIL STONE
   • Affects: Basic Right Tail barrier
   • Effect: Blocks 3 hits (up from 1), lasts 5 seconds (up from 2)
   • Quest to unlock: Block 30 enemy attacks with the barrier

7. PARRY STONE
   • Affects: Parry timing window
   • Effect: +0.1 seconds (0.4s becomes 0.5s)
   • Quest to unlock: Successfully parry 30 enemy attacks

8. COUNTER STONE
   • Affects: Damage after successful parry
   • Effect: +50% damage (60 becomes 90)
   • Quest to unlock: Defeat 15 kill points worth of enemies using parry counter

9. FLURRY STONE
   • Affects: Flurry Rush after perfect dodge
   • Effect: +2 extra hits (6 becomes 8)
   • Quest to unlock: Perform 15 successful Flurry Rushes

10. SPEED STONE
    • Affects: Movement speed
    • Effect: +10% running speed
    • Quest to unlock: Run 5000 meters total distance

STONE SLOTS:

You start with 3 slots. Maximum is 10 slots.

To get more slots, collect MAGATAMA:
• 0 Magatama = 3 slots
• 3 Magatama = 4 slots
• 6 Magatama = 5 slots
• 9 Magatama = 6 slots
• 12 Magatama = 7 slots

• 18 Magatama = 9 slots

There are 21 Magatama hidden throughout the world. Every 3 you find gives +1 slot.


RING ABILITIES — HOW THEY WORK

Rings are earned by defeating bosses:
• Defeat boss through COMBAT → Dark Soul → Left Ring
• Defeat boss through PERSUASION → Light Soul → Right Ring

When you get a ring, you can spend it on ONE of:
• Unlock a new ability (from the available pool)
• Upgrade an ability you already have

There are 5 Dark abilities and 5 Light abilities. Each can be upgraded once.


DARK PATH ABILITIES (Left Tail) — DETAILED

These abilities deal damage and are offensive. All use cooldowns only — no resource cost.


ABILITY 1: SOUL BURN

What it is: A charged fireball that burns enemies.

BASE VERSION:
• How it works: Yoru charges and throws a blue-purple fireball
• Direct hit damage: 30
• Burn effect: 5 damage per second for 3 seconds (15 total)
• Total damage: 45
• Cooldown: 8 seconds
• Animation: Ability_LeftTail_Slow (takes time to charge)
• Best used when: Enemy is staggered or distracted (slow animation)

UPGRADED VERSION (Soul Inferno):
• Same as base, PLUS:
• Explosion on impact: Deals 20 damage to all enemies within 3 meters
• Great for groups of enemies


ABILITY 2: SPIRIT SURGE

What it is: A power boost that eliminates all cooldowns temporarily.

BASE VERSION:
• How it works: Activating this immediately resets ALL your ability cooldowns
• Duration: For 4 seconds, you have NO COOLDOWNS (spam any ability freely)
• Cooldown: 90 seconds (very long — save for important fights)
• Visual: Yoru glows with crackling dark energy
• Best used when: Boss fights, emergencies, tough enemy groups

UPGRADED VERSION:
• Duration increases: 8 seconds of no cooldowns (doubled)
• Cooldown reduces: 75 seconds

Think of it like the Star power-up in Mario Kart — temporary invincibility to cooldowns.


ABILITY 3: NEKOMATA'S CALL (Necromancy)

What it is: Summon a ghost of a previously defeated enemy to fight for you.

HOW NECROMANCY WORKS:
1. First, defeat an enemy type at least once
2. Pay Mon (currency) to UNLOCK that enemy's soul for summoning
3. When you use Nekomata's Call, choose which enemy to summon
4. The ghost appears and fights for you
5. Ghost drains your SHINE continuously while active
6. Ghost disappears when: killed, Shine runs out, or you dismiss it

BASE VERSION:
• Summon cooldown: 45 seconds (between summons)
• Ghost HP: 50% of original enemy's HP
• Ghost damage: 75% of original enemy's damage
• Ghost follows you within 15 meters
• Shine drain: Continuous while ghost exists

UPGRADED VERSION (Undying Legion):
• Shine efficiency: Drains 50% slower (ghosts last twice as long)
• Can maintain ghosts more easily

WHAT ENEMIES CAN YOU SUMMON?
• Tier 4 (Common): 2 Soul Gems to unlock (1 with upgrade)
• Tier 3 (Mixed): 4 Soul Gems to unlock (2 with upgrade)
• Tier 2 (Guardians): 6 Soul Gems to unlock (3 with upgrade)
• Tier 1 (Bosses): CANNOT be summoned — too powerful

ONE SOUL PER TYPE:
If you kill 12 Kodama, you only store 1 Kodama soul. You can summon that Kodama type, but you don't get 12 summons.


ABILITY 4: INFERNO SPIRAL

What it is: A spinning fire attack that hits ALL enemies around you.

BASE VERSION:
• How it works: Yoru spins surrounded by fire
• Damage: 60 to EVERY enemy within range
• Range: 4 meters radius (centered on Yoru)
• Duration: 1.2 seconds (spinning animation)
• Cooldown: 15 seconds

UPGRADED VERSION (Hellfire Ring):
• Same spin attack, PLUS:
• Leaves burning ground for 5 seconds after
• Burning ground deals 10 damage per second
• Enemies standing in fire take up to 50 extra damage

DIFFERENT FROM COMBO 3:
• Combo 3 (spin): Hits ONE enemy, forward rush
• Inferno Spiral: Hits ALL enemies, stationary spin, fire element


ABILITY 5: SPIRIT JUDGMENT (Dark Ultimate)

What it is: Slow time, mark enemies, teleport to each and execute them.

CHARGE REQUIREMENT:
Before you can use this, you must charge it to 100% through combat:
• Basic attack hit: +2% charge
• Perfect dodge: +3% charge
• Parry success: +5% charge
• Enemy kill: +10% charge

HOW IT WORKS:
PHASE 1 — ACTIVATION (Hold the ultimate button):
• Time slows to 10% speed (almost frozen)
• You have 3 seconds in this state

PHASE 2 — MARKING (While holding button):
• Click on enemies to mark them
• Maximum marks: 5 (7 when upgraded)
• Marks appear as glowing symbols on enemies

PHASE 3 — EXECUTION (Release button):
• Yoru teleports to each marked enemy and strikes
• Each strike deals 40-50 damage (depends on karma)
• Total potential damage: 200-250 (280-350 upgraded)

COST: Using Spirit Judgment costs 1 Peach (you sacrifice health for power — Dark path)

BASE VERSION:
• Max marks: 5
• Damage per mark: 40-50
• Total damage: 200-250
• Cooldown: 60 seconds

UPGRADED VERSION (Eclipse Wrath):
• Max marks: 7
• Total damage: 280-350
• Cooldown: 60 seconds

KARMA EFFECT ON DAMAGE:
• More Left rings: Marks glow crimson, 50 damage each
• More Right rings: Marks glow gold, 40 damage each (less lethal Light path)
• Balanced: Marks glow purple, 45 damage each


LIGHT PATH ABILITIES (Right Tail) — DETAILED

These abilities are supportive and utility-focused. They deal NO DAMAGE. Light path = non-violent.
All use cooldowns only — no resource cost.


ABILITY 1: PURIFICATION BURST

What it is: A pulse that stuns enemies and opens them to dialogue.

BASE VERSION:
• How it works: Golden light pulses outward from Yoru
• Damage: 0 (NO damage — Light path)
• Effect: All enemies within range are STUNNED
• Stun duration: 2 seconds
• Range: 5 meters radius
• Cooldown: 10 seconds
• Special: During stun, a "Speak" prompt appears on stunned enemies

UPGRADED VERSION (Cleansing Wave):
• Stun duration: 3 seconds (longer window to talk)

ABILITY 2: CALMING MIST (Kiri)

What it is: A mist that makes enemies forget the battle happened.

"Kiri" means mist (霧) in Japanese.

BASE VERSION:
• How it works: Soft fog spreads from Yoru
• Damage: 0 (NO damage — Light path)
• Effect: Target enemy completely forgets the fight
• Result: Enemy returns to LostSoul state (passive, confused)
• Use: Failed a persuasion? Use this to reset and try again!
• Range: 5 meters (affects 1 enemy)
• Cooldown: 20 seconds
• Limitation: Cannot reset an enemy below 50% HP

UPGRADED VERSION:
• Range: 10 meters
• Can affect multiple enemies at once


ABILITY 3: HEALING WHISPER

What it is: Heal yourself over time.

BASE VERSION:
• How it works: Green and gold particles surround Yoru
• Healing: 2 HP over 3 seconds
• Equivalent to: Half a Peach restored
• Cooldown: 15 seconds
• Note: You can move and fight while healing

UPGRADED VERSION (Rejuvenation):
• Healing: 4 HP over 3 seconds (one full Peach)
• Bonus: Also removes negative effects (burns, poison, slow)


ABILITY 4: SOUL SIGHT

What it is: See hidden things in the world.

BASE VERSION:
• How it works: Yoru's eyes glow, world highlights change
• Damage: 0 (NO damage — Light path)
• Reveals: Hidden paths, secret items, breakable walls, enemy weak points
• Duration: 10 seconds
• Cooldown: 25 seconds
• Visual: Hidden objects glow with ethereal light

UPGRADED VERSION (True Vision):
• Duration: 20 seconds (doubled)
• Bonus: Enemy attack telegraphs glow brighter and earlier
• Use: Makes parrying and dodging easier during True Vision


ABILITY 5: ECLIPSE MERCY (Light Ultimate)

What it is: Mark yourself and heal massively.

CHARGE REQUIREMENT:
Same as Spirit Judgment — charge to 100% through combat.

HOW IT WORKS:
• Activate the ultimate
• Golden marks appear on Yoru (not enemies)
• Each mark heals 4 HP
• Maximum marks: 5 (7 when upgraded)
• Total healing: 20 HP = 5 full Peaches! (28 HP upgraded)

COST: FREE! (Light path mercy — no sacrifice required)

BASE VERSION:
• Max marks: 5
• Healing per mark: 4 HP
• Total healing: 20 HP (5 Peaches)
• Cooldown: 60 seconds

UPGRADED VERSION (Divine Restoration):
• Max marks: 7
• Total healing: 28 HP (7 Peaches)
• Bonus: 3 seconds of invincibility after healing completes


COMPARING THE TWO ULTIMATES:

Spirit Judgment (Dark):
• Purpose: Kill enemies
• Effect: Up to 350 damage
• Cost: Lose 1 Peach
• Best for: Aggressive combat players

Eclipse Mercy (Light):
• Purpose: Heal yourself
• Effect: Up to 28 HP healing
• Cost: FREE
• Best for: Survival-focused players

Both require 100% charge and have 60 second cooldowns.


NECROMANCY SYSTEM
Puppet Master Mechanic
CONCEPT: Yoru can summon defeated enemy souls to fight alongside her!
Core Mechanics
Collecting Bodies:
STEP 1: Defeat Enemy (First Time)
- Enemy drops soul essence
- Popup: "New Soul Unlocked!"
- Added to Necromancy Grimoire (locked)

STEP 2: Purchase Soul Control
- Open Grimoire [Hold R]
- Select locked soul
- Pay ONE-TIME FEE: 300 Mon + 3 Shine
- Soul permanently unlocked for summoning!

STEP 3: Summon Puppet
- Open Grimoire → Select unlocked soul
- Pay activation cost: 2-5 Shine (depends on enemy strength)
- Puppet spawns beside player

TORI GATE SYSTEM

How to reach a boss:

STEP 1: FIND A TORI GATE
There are 10 Tori Gates in the world, one for each boss.
Gates are locked — you cannot enter without offerings.

STEP 2: DEFEAT OR PERSUADE THE GUARDIANS
Tier 2 Guardians guard the offerings near each gate.
You can fight them (Combat) or talk to them (Persuasion).
Both give the same reward.

STEP 3: COLLECT THE OFFERINGS
Each Guardian drops one type of offering:
• Omamori (amulet)
• Incense
• Sake

To open a gate, you need ALL THREE types:
• 1 Omamori + 1 Incense + 1 Sake

STEP 4: OPEN THE GATE
Bring your offerings to the Tori Gate.
Use them to unlock the gate.
A portal opens.

STEP 5: ENTER THE BOSS ARENA
Walk through the portal.
A separate scene loads.
Boss encounter begins — choose Combat or Persuasion.


COMBAT VS PERSUASION REWARDS

IMPORTANT: Both paths give EQUAL rewards!
Choose based on preference and roleplay, not efficiency.

Combat Path:
• You fight and defeat the enemy
• Enemy dies and drops items on the ground
• Soul is RED (Dark)

Persuasion Path:
• You talk to the enemy and help them find peace
• Enemy GIFTS you the same items before ascending into light
• Soul is BLUE (Light)

The rewards are identical. The only difference is the soul color (which determines your ring type).


ENEMY STATE MACHINE

Every enemy (except bosses) starts in the same state and can change based on your actions:

STATE 1: LOSTSOUL (Default)
• Enemy is passive, confused, wandering
• NOT aggressive — won't attack you
• If you ATTACK: Enemy becomes Hostile
• If you TALK: Enemy enters Dialogue

STATE 2: DIALOGUE
• Persuasion interaction is active
• Mini-game or dialogue choices appear
• If SUCCESS: Enemy becomes Peaceful
• If FAIL: Enemy becomes Hostile IMMEDIATELY (no second chance!)

STATE 3: HOSTILE
• Combat is active, enemy fights you
• Enemy attacks until one of you is defeated
• If you DEFEAT enemy: Enemy becomes Dead
• If you RUN 15+ meters away: Enemy returns to LostSoul
• WARNING: Once Hostile, you CANNOT return to Dialogue!

STATE 4: PEACEFUL
• Enemy found peace and is ascending into light
• Gives you Mon and items as a gift
• IMMUNE to damage — you cannot attack them
• Dissolves into beautiful light particles

STATE 5: DEAD
• Enemy was defeated through combat
• Drops loot on the ground
• Corpse remains for 60 seconds, then disappears

KEY RULES:
• Your FIRST action determines the path (attack = Hostile, talk = Dialogue)
• Once Hostile, dialogue is LOCKED (unless you use Calming Mist ability)
• To try again differently: Reload your last checkpoint

BALANCE SYSTEM
BALANCE SYSTEM
[New Section — Complete Technical Design from Balance System Handoff v2.0]

This section explains how the world transforms based on your moral choices. This is the heart of YORU — the world itself reacts to how you play.


CORE CONCEPT

In YORU, your choices are VISIBLE. The two tails show your karma:
• LEFT TAIL: Dark/Shadow energy (judgment, punishment, night)
• RIGHT TAIL: Light/Divine energy (mercy, forgiveness, day)

Each tail can have 0 to 10 glowing rings. These rings are earned from bosses:
• Combat victory → Dark Soul → Ring on LEFT tail
• Persuasion victory → Light Soul → Ring on RIGHT tail

The BALANCE is calculated as:
Balance = Right Rings minus Left Rings

Range: -10 (pure dark) to +10 (pure light)

Examples:
• 0 Left, 0 Right = Balance 0 (Neutral — game start)
• 5 Left, 0 Right = Balance -5 (Dark path)
• 0 Left, 5 Right = Balance +5 (Light path)
• 5 Left, 5 Right = Balance 0 (ECLIPSE — both high but balanced)
• 10 Left, 0 Right = Balance -10 (Pure Dark)
• 0 Left, 10 Right = Balance +10 (Pure Light)

Moon phase changes with rings:
• 3 Left rings: Quarter moon
• 4 Left rings: Nearly full moon
• 5+ Left rings: Full moon


STATE PRIORITY (How the game decides which state to use):

When rings change, the game checks in this order:
1. Is this Eclipse? (difference ≤ 1 AND both tails have 2+ rings)
2. Is this a Sunset/Sunrise transition?
3. Is this Escalation? (6+ rings on one side)
4. Is this a Path state? (difference > 2)
5. Is this mild dark/light? (small difference)
6. Default to Neutral

Besides weather and visuals:

• Dark path: Tree withers, loses leaves, looks dying
• Light path: Tree flourishes, blooms, looks healthy
