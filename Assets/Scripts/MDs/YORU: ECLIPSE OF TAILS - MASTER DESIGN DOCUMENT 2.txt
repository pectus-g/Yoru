CORE CONCEPT
High Concept
Play as Yoru, a two-tailed Nekomata navigating purgatory. Every moral choice is literally visible on your body through glowing rings on your tails. Your decisions shape both your appearance and the entire world around you.
Unique Selling Points
Visible Karma: Rings on tails show your moral path (left = dark/combat, right = light/persuasion)
Dual Forms: Transform between Nekomata (cat) and Old Woman (Tomoe) to see emotional auras
World Responsiveness: Environment changes dynamically based on ring distribution (7 possible world states)
Buddhist Themes: Each boss represents one of the 10 Fetters (attachments) from Buddhist philosophy
Authentic Japanese Folklore: All bosses based on real yokai with cultural respect
Core Themes
Grief and acceptance
Duality (life/death, light/dark, forgiveness/vengeance)
Breaking attachments to find peace
Memory and identity
The weight of choices

GAME LOOP
Complete Progression Flow
1. START AT ANCIENT TREE
   ↓
2. TREE GIVES QUEST
   - "I sense a tormented spirit in the Northern Forest..."
   - Quest marker appears on map
   ↓
3. EXPLORE TO FIND BOSS
   - Discover map by walking
   - Fight small enemies (Tier 4)
   - Collect Mon (currency)
   - Find offering guardians (Tier 2)
   ↓
4. REACH TORI GATE
   - Requires 1 Omamori + 1 Incense + 1 Sake to open
   - Gate leads to boss realm (separate scene)
   ↓
5. BOSS ENCOUNTER
   - Short cinematic (30 sec - shows corrupted state)
   - Choose path:
   
   PATH A: COMBAT                    PATH B: PERSUASION
   - Fight boss                      - Transform to human
   - Reduce HP to 0                  - See emotional aura
   - Fast, agile combat              - Emotional Threading mini-game
   - Dodge, parry, combo             - Match correct emotion path
   ↓                                 ↓
6. BOSS DEFEATED
   - Drops soul orb (red if combat, blue if persuasion)
   - Drops 500-1000 Mon
   - Drops 3-5 unique consumables
   - Drops special item (boss-themed)
   ↓
7. COLLECT SOUL
   - Walk near soul orb
   - Absorption animation plays
   - Soul flies to player like magnet
   - Added to soul inventory
   ↓
8. RETURN TO ANCIENT TREE
   - Tree glows (ready to free soul)
   - Press [E] in tree's area
   ↓
9. FREEING SOUL RITUAL
   - Soul inventory opens
   - Select which soul to free
   - Full cinematic plays (1 min flashback)
   - Shows boss's backstory (Tomoe's memory)
   - Emotional resolution
   ↓
10. RECEIVE RING
    - Tree rewards ring item to inventory
    - Auto-save creates restore point
    ↓
11. ACTIVATE RING (FORCED)
    - Must activate to progress
    - "Circle Activation" animation plays
    - Ring becomes visible on tail (XFur color 0 → visible)
    - Ability tree opens
    ↓
12. CHOOSE ABILITY
    - Combat soul → 10 left-tail abilities available
    - Persuasion soul → 10 right-tail abilities available
    - Pick ONE ability (permanent)
    - 9 abilities remain for future rings
    ↓
13. WORLD SHIFTS
    - Environment changes based on new ring ratio
    - Skybox adjusts (sun/moon/eclipse)
    - NPCs react differently
    - Music transitions
    ↓
14. STATS INCREASE
    - Peaches: +1 every 2 rings (3→4→5→6→7→8)
    - Shines: +1 every 2 rings (3→4→5→6→7→8)
    ↓
15. NEXT QUEST
    - Tree reveals next boss location
    - Repeat steps 3-14 for all 10 bosses
    ↓
16. AFTER 10 BOSSES
    - Check ring balance
    - If 5L-5R (perfect balance) → Secret realm unlocks!
    - Core Realm (Izanagi & Izanami) revealed
    ↓
17. ENDING
    - One of 7 endings based on ring distribution
    - Post-game content unlocks

PERSUASION SYSTEM
Emotional Threading Mini-Game
CONCEPT: Weave a thread of understanding through the enemy's tangled emotions to reach their heart.
How It Works
Visual:
Screen shows enemy's "emotional web" - like a constellation/neural network
Multiple emotion nodes (glowing orbs) connected by potential paths
Each node has a color matching emotional state
Center = HEART (goal)

Example for Yuki-onna (GRIEF):

    [ANGER-Red]     [FEAR-Yellow]
         ↓         ↙
    [GRIEF-Blue] → [GUILT-Purple] → [HOPE-White] → ❤️ HEART
         ↑         ↘
    [DESPAIR-Gray]  [REGRET-Purple]
Gameplay:
Player sees 8-10 emotion nodes scattered on screen
Must draw a line connecting nodes in correct order
Click/drag mouse or use arrow keys to trace path
Some connections work (nodes light up green)
Some connections break thread (nodes flash red, try again)
Reach heart = Persuasion success!
Limited attempts (3 tries per boss)
Difficulty Scaling:
BASED ON DARK RING COUNT:

Easy (0-2 dark rings):
- 3 correct nodes in straight line
- 5 total nodes (2 wrong)
- Clear emotional logic

Medium (3-5 dark rings):
- 5 correct nodes
- 8 total nodes (3 wrong)
- Some branching paths

Hard (6-8 dark rings):
- 7 correct nodes
- 12 total nodes (5 wrong)
- Multiple dead-end paths
- Time limit (60 seconds)

Very Hard (9-10 dark rings):
- 8 correct nodes
- 15 total nodes (7 wrong)
- Complex web, many dead ends
- Time limit (45 seconds)
Example Correct Paths:
Yuki-onna (Grief): GRIEF → GUILT → REGRET → HOPE → HEART
Oni (Rage): RAGE → EMPATHY → CALM → UNDERSTANDING → HEART
Gashadokuro (Despair): DESPAIR → ACKNOWLEDGMENT → PURPOSE → HOPE → HEART
Success/Failure
Success:
Thread reaches heart
Beautiful particle effect
Enemy calms, becomes peaceful
Drops blue soul orb (persuasion)
+1 right tail ring potential
Failure (3 attempts used):
Thread cannot reach heart
Enemy becomes hostile
Forces combat encounter
If defeated in combat, drops red soul orb
+1 left tail ring potential
Partial Success (2 attempts):
Can try again with hints
Some nodes glow slightly (guidance)

Parry System
Timing: Press [Space] exactly when enemy attack lands (0.2 sec window)
Success:
Enemy attack deflected with particle burst
Enemy stunned (2 seconds)
Yoru can counter with free combo
Shine +1 (reward for skill!)
Slow-motion flash (0.5 sec)
Screen flash effect
Perfect Parry: (0.1 sec window - very tight!)
Enemy stunned longer (4 seconds)
Shine +2
Golden flash particle effect
Achievement unlocked: "Perfect Reflexes"
Failure:
If timed early: Normal dodge
If timed late: Take damage
Jump vs Parry Detection:
if (Input.GetKeyDown(Space))
{
    if (isNearEnemy && enemyIsAttacking)
        → Parry attempt
    else
        → Jump
}

Context-sensitive based on enemy proximity and attack state!

NECROMANCY SYSTEM
Puppet Master Mechanic
CONCEPT: Yoru can summon defeated enemy souls to fight alongside her!
Core Mechanics
Collecting Bodies:
STEP 1: Defeat Enemy (First Time)
- Enemy drops soul essence
- Popup: "New Soul Unlocked!"
- Added to Necromancy Grimoire (locked)

STEP 2: Purchase Soul Control
- Open Grimoire [Hold R]
- Select locked soul
- Pay ONE-TIME FEE: 300 Mon + 3 Shine
- Soul permanently unlocked for summoning!

STEP 3: Summon Puppet
- Open Grimoire → Select unlocked soul
- Pay activation cost: 2-5 Shine (depends on enemy strength)
- Puppet spawns beside player
- Begins draining Shine continuously

Puppet Behavior

AGGRESSIVE MODE:

DEFENSIVE MODE:

Shine Drain System
Balanced Drain Rates:
PUPPET STRENGTH → DRAIN RATE:

Weak enemies (Tier 4):
- Drain: 0.1 Shine/sec
- Duration: 80 seconds (with 8 Shine)

Medium enemies (Tier 3):
- Drain: 0.2 Shine/sec
- Duration: 40 seconds

Strong enemies (Tier 2):
- Drain: 0.3 Shine/sec
- Duration: 26 seconds

MULTIPLE PUPPETS:
- Drain stacks additively
- 3 weak puppets = 0.3 Shine/sec total
- 5 weak puppets = 0.5 Shine/sec total
- 2 strong puppets = 0.6 Shine/sec total

MAXIMUM PUPPETS: 5 simultaneously
Puppet Health
Visual:
Small health bar above each puppet's head:

[Puppet Name] ████████░░ 80%

Color-coded:
- Green: 100-70% HP
- Yellow: 69-30% HP
- Red: 29-0% HP (critical)

When HP reaches 0:
- Puppet vanishes in particle poof
- Stop draining Shine
- Can resummon immediately (if have Shine)
Puppet dies when:
HP depleted by enemy attacks
Shine reaches 0 (all puppets disappear!)
Player manually dismisses (hold R, click dismiss)

20 abilities total, but player can only unlock 10!

Ring 1 (Combat soul from Yuki-onna):

- Choose "Frozen Claws" (ice damage)
- 9 combat abilities remain

Ring 2 (Persuasion soul from Oni):

- Choose "Peaceful Flame" (calming aura)
- 9 persuasion abilities remain

Ring 3 (Combat soul):
- Open left pool (9 options remaining)
- Choose "Soul Burn" (DoT damage)
- 8 combat abilities remain

...continue until 10 rings activated

Final Result: Player has 10 abilities total
Could be: 7 combat + 3 persuasion
OR: 5 combat + 5 persuasion
OR: 10 combat + 0 persuasion
OR: Any combination!

WORLD RESPONSIVENESS
7 World States Based on Ring Distribution
THE BIG MECHANIC: World changes dynamically based on player's moral choices!
CALCULATION:

leftRings = 0 to 10
rightRings = 0 to 10

if (leftRings > rightRings + 2):
    worldState = DARK
else if (rightRings > leftRings + 2):
    worldState = LIGHT
else if (leftRings == rightRings):
    worldState = ECLIPSE (PERFECT BALANCE)
else:
    worldState = BALANCED (slightly leaning)

Apply world state to all systems!

SMOOTH, NOT SUDDEN!
Implementation Difficulty
MEDIUM - Mostly asset swapping and color grading
Development Time: 5-7 days
Worth it? ABSOLUTELY! This is the core unique mechanic!
Technical Approach
void UpdateWorldState()
{
    int leftRings = GetLeftRingCount();
    int rightRings = GetRightRingCount();
    
    // Calculate state
    WorldState newState = CalculateWorldState(leftRings, rightRings);
    
    // Apply changes
    UpdateSkybox(newState);
    UpdateLighting(newState);
    UpdateFoliage(newState);
    UpdateNPCs(newState);
    UpdateMusic(newState);
    UpdateTree(newState);
    
    // Smooth transitions
    StartCoroutine(TransitionWorldState(currentState, newState, 3f));
}
