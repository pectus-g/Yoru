# YORU: Eclipse of Tails
# Video reference shot list for the sound designer and composer

Pectus Games. Prepared 13 Sep 2026.
Companion to `YORU_AUDIO_ORDER_ONI_FIGHT.md`.

Record this list, send the clips with the order sheet. Section numbers match the order sheet.

---

## RECORDING SETTINGS

- **Mute the game audio.** Placeholder sound anchors the designer to the wrong thing.
- 1080p, 60 fps if your capture allows, otherwise 30.
- Record in the Oni cave scene, night, so the lighting matches the final look.
- One file per numbered shot below. Name the file with the shot number and name,
  for example `03_swing_light.mp4`. That is how they will match video to the order sheet.
- Perform each move **2 or 3 times** with a second of stillness between takes, so they can
  hear the natural gap.
- Do not narrate over the clips. Put notes in this document instead.

## H. NOTES TO SEND WITH THE CLIPS

Write a short paragraph covering:

- Yoru is a **small light cat**, not a heavy character. Her footsteps are soft pads.
  She wears a **collar with a bell**.
- She has **two tails** that fire magic.
- She can transform into a **grandmother (Granny)** form.
- The Oni is **enormous**, carries a **kanabo**, an iron studded club.
- The fight has **two phases**. Phase 2 is faster and harder, **not darker or heavier**.
  Escalation reads as tempo, not gloom.
- Reference for the overall feel: the game **Ori**, artistically deep, cozy but cold.
- Parry is a core mechanic. The **parry clang** is the signature sound of the fight.
